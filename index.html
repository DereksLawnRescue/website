About

Hey, I'm Derek

Derek's Community Rescue started with a setback. I was driving a semi truck for the Postal Service when I was in a serious accident. It wasn't my fault, but it still cost me my job, and that paycheck was my family's only income.

With my last $500, I bought a cheap push mower, a string trimmer and a broom, and started mowing lawns to keep food on the table. That small start grew into the lawn care business I still run today.

Doing that work, I kept seeing yards that had gotten away from people. Behind almost every one was someone going through something hard. I knew what it was like to have life knock you down, so I started showing up.

Every cleanup is completely free for the homeowner, no questions asked. I mow, trim, haul it all off, and film the whole thing start to finish, so you can see the mess, the work, and the moment the homeowner sees their yard again.
